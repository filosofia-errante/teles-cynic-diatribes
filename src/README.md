# Teles: The Cynic Teacher

Teles (fl. mid-3rd century BCE) was a Greek teacher and wandering philosopher whose surviving diatribes represent the earliest continuous examples of Cynic writing in antiquity.

Because the original treatises of Diogenes of Sinope, Crates of Thebes, and Bion of Borysthenes have perished, Teles serves as an indispensable record of early Cynic pedagogy, street-corner wit, and practical ethics.
